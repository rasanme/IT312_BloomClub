About Us

Bloom Club is a small creative space for people who love flowers. We offer simple, fun activities like bouquet arrangement and perfume preparation, all designed to help members relax, learn, and enjoy their time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread creativity and joy through easy, relaxing workshops. We want to help people reduce stress, learn new skills, and connect with others in a positive environment.
